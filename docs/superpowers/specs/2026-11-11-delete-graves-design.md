# Design: Deleting Graves, and a Page of Inactive Graves

**Created**: 2026-11-11

**Status**: Draft, waiting for the owner's approval

**Source**: GitHub issue #68 ("erlaube das „löschen“ von gräbern") and the
owner's answers of 2026-11-11.

## 1. Intent

Some graves should never have existed: records the FriedWin import brought
in by mistake, or graves created wrongly in the app — sometimes noticed only
after they were invoiced. The owner needs to take such a grave out of the
app entirely: out of every list, never invoiced again, and — together with
its invoices — out of anything that counts, such as statistics the app will
get later. Because such a mistake can itself be a mistake, a deleted grave
can be restored, but only as long as no invoice run has happened since, so
a restored grave never misses a half-year.

Separately, ended graves (real graves whose term is over) leave the lists of
current work and get a place of their own.

## 2. Decisions

| Topic                          | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deleting is marking            | A grave is not removed from the database. It gets a deletion mark with the time it was deleted (`DeletedAt`), and its invoices stay stored (invoice numbers are never reused).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Where the action is            | On the grave's detail page, for ordinary and cooperative graves alike ("Grab löschen").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Confirmation                   | Always. The dialog explains the consequences: the grave disappears from every list and from statistics, it is never invoiced again, its invoices no longer count anywhere, and it can be restored only until the next invoice run. The owner confirms explicitly.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| A deleted grave                | Is never invoiced (left out of every run like a cooperative grave, and refused by the generator as a last guard). Cannot be edited, handed over or revised (Claude's call); its detail page stays readable, with its invoices, and shows that it is deleted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Its invoices                   | Stop counting anywhere: they leave the half-year overview's counts, the bulk ZIP downloads, payment marking and every list; they stay readable on the deleted grave's page (Claude's call), so nothing is lost.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Restoring                      | "Wiederherstellen" on the deleted grave's page, while no invoice run (a completed half-year generation, for any half-year) has happened since `DeletedAt`; afterwards the action is gone and a sentence says why. A restored grave is exactly what it was, invoices included, and they count again (Claude's call). Restoring is refused when another grave with the same number now overlaps its term (FR-018a), because a deleted grave's number is free for a new grave in the meantime (Claude's call). |
| Lists                          | A deleted grave appears in no list but the new page: not in the grave overview, the list "Genossenschaftsgräber", the item dialog ("graves with this item") or any picker.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Ended graves                   | A grave is ended once its term ended before the current half-year. The grave overview needs no change: it shows one half-year, and an ended grave appears only in half-years inside its term. The list "Genossenschaftsgräber" shows a cooperative grave only when its term reaches into the current half-year or later; its "Laufzeit" select is removed.                                                                                                                                                  |
| New page                       | "Inaktive Gräber" in the navigation, after "Genossenschaftsgräber", with four panels: "Beendete Gräber", "Gelöschte Gräber", "Beendete Genossenschaftsgräber", "Gelöschte Genossenschaftsgräber". Each lists grave number, site name, cemetery and term (and the contract number for cooperative graves, the deletion date for deleted ones), with a text search, and opens the grave's detail page. A deleted grave is listed only under "Gelöscht", never also under "Beendet".                           |
| Where invoices are filtered    | Grave invoices have no list of their own today (the invoice list shows only invoices without a grave). What has to leave out a deleted grave's invoices: the half-year overview and its counts, the bulk ZIP downloads, payment marking (refused for such an invoice), revisions (refused), and any endpoint that returns grave invoices. Its grave invoice detail page (`/graves/:id/invoices/:rootId`) still opens, read-only (Claude's call).                                                            |
| Marking invoices               | No mark on the invoice itself: an invoice belongs to a deleted grave when its grave has `DeletedAt` set, so restoring needs to touch nothing but the grave (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| What closes the restore window | A grave-invoice generation job that succeeded after `DeletedAt`, for any half-year and whatever it generated. A failed or cancelled run does not close it (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Grave numbers                  | A deleted grave does not count in the overlap check (FR-018a) of other graves, so its number is free; a deleted grave cannot be handed over. The import always goes into an empty database, so it never meets a deleted grave.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Recipients and items           | Unchanged: a recipient or item used only by deleted graves stays as it is, and the item locks keep counting every link (Claude's call; nothing in the app deletes items or recipients).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Restore and delete again       | Restoring clears `DeletedAt`; a restored grave can be deleted again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| API                            | `DeleteGrave` and `RestoreGrave` as actions beside `SaveGrave` (the `Graves` set takes no writes). The `Graves` set exposes `DeletedAt`; every list asks for what it needs (the cooperative list adds "not deleted"). The new page filters in the browser like the other lists, using the browser's current half-year as they do.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Tests of restoring             | The restore window depends on whether any run happened since, which parallel E2E specs change at will. It is tested in integration tests; the E2E specs cover deleting, the dialog, the lists and the new page (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Statistics                     | The app has none yet; deleted graves and their invoices are marked so that later statistics leave them out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Migration                      | One new migration for the next version (constitution IV), shared with anything else that version adds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## 3. Non-goals

- Removing a grave or its invoices from the database.
- Cancelling (Storno) invoices that were already sent; the owner handles that outside the app.
- Statistics themselves.

## 4. Left to the builder

- How "an invoice run since the deletion" is read from the stored data (e.g. a completed grave-invoice job finished after `DeletedAt`).
- The German wording of the dialog and messages beyond the names above.
- Whether the four panels are an accordion or stacked cards.
