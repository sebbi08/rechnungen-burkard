# Design: Quotes (Angebote)

**Created**: 2026-11-12

**Status**: Approved design (pre-implementation), approved by the owner on 2026-11-12

**Source**: GitHub issue #70 ("Angebotserstellung") and the owner's answers of
2026-11-12.

## 1. Intent

Before some jobs the owner sends the customer a quote. It often goes back and
forth a few times — the customer asks for changes, the owner sends a new
version — until the customer either declines or accepts. On acceptance the
quote becomes an invoice. Today the app has no place for this, so quotes live
outside it and the invoice is typed in again.

The app gets a section for quotes that works like the one for ordinary
invoices: the same input, the same printout apart from its title, revisions for
every round of changes. A quote is kept when the customer declines, so the
owner can look up later what was offered. One click turns the latest version of
an accepted quote into an invoice.

## 2. Decisions

"Owner" marks what the owner decided; "Claude's call" marks a decision the
owner did not make and can overturn while reading.

| Topic              | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What a quote is    | A recipient and one or more lines, entered exactly like an ordinary (non-grave) invoice: the same recipient picker with quick create, item picker, count, optional price and the VAT-included switch per line, the same validation (an address is required, prices to the cent, at least one line) (owner: "selbe Eingabe wie bei Rechnungen"). Quotes exist for ordinary invoices only, never for grave invoices (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Quote numbers      | A numbering of their own, separate from invoices: `A2026-0001`, `A2026-0002`, …, per year from 1, the year being the local year the quote was created in (owner: the invoice number is fixed only when the invoice is created; format Claude's call). A quote number is never given twice, even after the quote with the highest number was deleted, since that quote may already have reached a customer (Claude's call). Invoice numbers are untouched: a quote never takes or reserves one, so invoice numbering stays gapless.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Rounds of changes  | Editing a quote creates a revision, as with invoices: `A2026-0001-R1`, `-R2`, …; every earlier version stays stored and downloadable (Claude's call, confirmed by the owner's summary). Only an open quote can be edited.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| The printout       | The invoice document with its title changed and nothing else (owner: "alles gleich nur oben nicht RECHNUNG sondern Angebot"). The head reads `**** ANGEBOT ****`; number, customer number, date and page as on an invoice; the same lines, VAT block, letterhead and footer; and the **same ending text from the settings** as invoices (owner). The PDF's metadata title and the line that names the document read "Angebot …" where an invoice's read "Rechnung …". The VAT block's wording, the ending text and the footer are not reworded for quotes. The date is the day the version was created.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| No copy            | A quote has one PDF only, no stamped copy (owner). Its file name is the quote number: `A2026-0001.pdf`, `A2026-0001-R1.pdf`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Snapshots          | Like an invoice, a quote version stores what it printed — recipient name, address and number, the lines with item number, description, count, net price, VAT rate and VAT-included flag, and the ending text — so it is reproducible from stored data (constitution II) and a later change to a recipient, item or the settings never alters a version already sent (Claude's call, mirrors invoices).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| States             | A quote (the whole chain of its versions) is **Offen**, **Abgelehnt** or **Umgewandelt** (owner: a declined quote is kept as declined). A new quote is open.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Declining          | "Ablehnen" sets an open quote to declined. A declined quote keeps all its versions for looking up; it cannot be edited or converted. "Wieder öffnen" sets it back to open, for a customer who comes back (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Converting         | "In Rechnung umwandeln" on an open quote creates the invoice straight away from the quote's **latest version**, with no editor in between (owner). One short confirmation comes first, naming the quote and saying that an invoice with the next invoice number is created and cannot be deleted, because a misclick on the wrong row would leave a permanent invoice (Claude's call). The invoice is an ordinary new invoice: it takes the next free invoice number at that moment, the date of that day, the current ending text and stamp word, and both PDFs (normal and copy), exactly like `CreateInvoice`. Its lines are the quote's lines as they were quoted — same items, counts, net prices, VAT rates and VAT-included flags — even where an item's price has changed since. Its recipient values are taken from the recipient as it is now, as every new invoice does (Claude's call). The quote becomes converted in the same transaction, so a quote can never be converted twice. The conversion shows the blocking progress indicator while the PDFs render (constitution VIII). Afterwards the app opens the new invoice's detail page (Claude's call). |
| After converting   | A converted quote is closed: no edits, no declining, no second conversion, no deleting (owner: deleting only without an invoice). The quote links to its invoice and the invoice links back to its quote, on both detail pages. The invoice is from then on an ordinary invoice: it can be revised and marked paid as before, and neither touches the quote. The invoice PDF does not mention the quote (Claude's call: "alles gleich").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Deleting           | An open or declined quote can be deleted, with all its versions and PDFs, after a confirmation (owner: deletable only while it has no invoice). Deleting is meant for mistakes — wrong customer, created twice — and removes the rows for good; declining is the normal way when a customer says no (Claude's call). A converted quote cannot be deleted; the server refuses it, not only the UI.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Navigation         | A new entry "Angebote" directly below "Rechnungen" (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Quote list         | Built like the invoice list (FR-066, FR-068 – FR-068d, FR-076): one row per quote showing its latest version — number, recipient, date, total (gross) and state — with a search per column, the state as a select (Alle / Offen / Abgelehnt / Umgewandelt), answered and paged by the server, 10 per page, searches in the URL query. "Neues Angebot" opens the editor. Each row offers what the quote's state allows: download the PDF, edit, convert, decline or reopen, delete; a converted row shows its invoice number linking to the invoice. The list shows every state unless filtered (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Quote detail page  | Like the invoice detail page: the state, every version with its download, the actions the state allows, and for a converted quote the link to its invoice. Route `/quotes/:rootId`, list `/quotes` (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Invoice side       | The invoice detail page of an invoice created from a quote shows "Aus Angebot A2026-0001" linking to the quote. The invoice list and everything else about invoices are unchanged; quotes appear in no invoice list, invoice search, bulk download or payment marking (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Storage            | Quotes are stored apart from invoices (own tables for quotes, their lines and their PDF), so nothing that reads invoices — number allocation, lists, bulk downloads, later statistics — can ever pick up a quote (Claude's call). The invoice gets a nullable reference to the quote it came from.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| API                | OData actions beside the invoice ones, e.g. `CreateQuote`, `CreateQuoteRevision`, `DeclineQuote`, `ReopenQuote`, `ConvertQuote`, `DeleteQuote`; a read-only entity set for the list and detail page; a download for the quote PDF. Each action refuses what the quote's state does not allow, with an error code the UI turns into a German message. Exact names are the builder's.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Backup and restore | The planned backup of all data (2026-11-12 backup design) covers quotes like every other table. Restoring an older backup gives newer quote numbers out again, as it does invoice numbers; the restore's summary and its warning about newer data count quotes as they count invoices (a quote count beside `Invoices`, and newer quotes also set the "newer data here" warning). Whichever of the two features lands second adds this (Claude's call).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Migration          | One new migration for the next version (constitution IV), shared with anything else that version adds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Spec               | `docs/spec.md` gets a user story for quotes and FRs for the decisions above.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Tests              | Integration tests for numbering (own sequence, per year, not reused after deleting the highest), conversion (lines copied as quoted, next invoice number, state change, refused twice), and the state guards on every action. E2E specs for creating, revising, declining and reopening, converting (landing on the invoice, links both ways), deleting, and the list's searches; the quote pages join the phone and tablet layout spec. Specs go through `e2e/vue.ts` as the others do.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## 3. Non-goals

- A validity date or any quote-specific wording on the printout (terms,
  "gültig bis", a different ending text).
- A stamped copy of a quote.
- Quotes for graves.
- Sending quotes by e-mail from the app.
- Converting a quote partially, or into several invoices.
- Reserving invoice numbers for quotes.

## 4. Anti-goals

- A gap in the invoice numbers caused by a quote.
- A quote showing up anywhere invoices are counted, listed or downloaded.
- A version that a customer received changing afterwards, by an edit, a
  changed item, recipient or setting.

## 5. Left to the builder

- How the never-reused quote numbers are kept (a counter per year, or the
  highest number including deleted ones recorded somewhere).
- Whether the quote PDF comes from the invoice document with a title parameter
  or from a shared base; the result has to match the invoice layout, page two
  included (see CLAUDE.md on the letterhead).
- How far `InvoiceEditor`, the row actions and the revision components are
  reused or generalised for quotes.
- The German wording of buttons, confirmations, state labels and error
  messages beyond the names above, and the colours of the state tags.
